# Design System Checker — UI Audit

**Catch the design inconsistencies AI-assisted UI can leave behind.**

Design System Checker by **Design OS** helps you spot where a Figma design drifts from the system your team already uses. Review hard-coded colors, off-brand typography, and inconsistent spacing—without manually inspecting every layer.

**🚀 Coming soon to Figma Community**

## Why Design OS?

Creating interfaces is faster than ever. Keeping them consistent is another story.

AI-generated and manually built screens alike can introduce colors that bypass variables, fonts that do not match your brand, and spacing that ignores your scale. These small decisions add up to inconsistent experiences and avoidable rework.

**Design OS turns those inconsistencies into clear, actionable findings**, so designers can spend less time hunting for problems and more time refining the experience.

## What it catches

| Design-system check | What Design OS flags | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Colors** | Fills and strokes that aren't bound to color variables | Find and bind a nearby matching variable |
| **Typography** | Fonts outside your approved brand families and text without an applied text style | Review the mismatch and use a suitable style when available |
| **Spacing** | Padding, gaps, and measurable edge insets outside your allowed spacing scale | Snap values to the nearest allowed step |

You define the **Brand fonts** and **Spacing** references, and Design OS checks selected designs against them. Color suggestions use the variables available to the file, including accessible team-library variables.

## A faster way to review UI

1. **Select a frame or section.** The audit runs automatically—no scan button and no whole-page sweep.
2. **See what needs attention.** Each finding shows the affected layer, the issue, why it matters, and a suggested fix.
3. **Fix with context.** Jump to the layer on the canvas and apply an available suggestion.
4. **Make feedback visible.** Choose **Mark on canvas** to annotate issues, then **Clear** to remove the marks added by Design OS.
5. **Share the review.** Use **Copy report** to bring actionable findings into design reviews, handoffs, or tickets.

## Built for practical design reviews

- **AI-assisted design QA:** catch off-system choices before generated UI becomes product UI.
- **Design-system adoption:** spot where teams have used raw values instead of existing foundations.
- **Handoff reviews:** share specific, layer-level findings instead of vague feedback.
- **Quick iteration:** switch between frames and get updated results automatically.

Design OS checks **design-system consistency**, not whether a screen was made by AI.

## Focused by design

- **Selection only.** Your review stays inside the frame or section you choose.
- **Your system is the reference.** Checks are based on available variables, text styles, approved fonts, and spacing values—not arbitrary visual opinions.
- **Annotations are optional.** Nothing is marked on the canvas unless you request it.
- **No external upload.** The plugin runs through Figma's plugin APIs and does not send design files to an external service.
